## Line 1 — Orient (Frame 1)

**Time:** 0.0 – 6.0s
**Delivery:** Warm, unhurried open.

    Welcome to your trial. Here's a 30-second orientation to how it works - not a product tour.

## Line 2 — Seats (Frame 2)

**Time:** 6.0 – 13.0s
**Delivery:** Matter-of-fact, a little brisk.

    Your trial has a fixed seat cap, set when it was issued. Every teammate who joins takes one of those seats - and every invite has to match your trial's own domain.

## Line 3 — Expiry & extension (Frame 3)

**Time:** 13.0 – 21.0s
**Delivery:** Steady, slightly more deliberate on "automatically torn down."

    Your trial expires on a set date - shown right in the systray. Past that date, it's automatically torn down. Need more time? Ask your sales contact for an extension before it lapses.

## Line 4 — Invites (Frame 4)

**Time:** 21.0 – 28.0s
**Delivery:** Clear enumeration, two distinct paths.

    You can invite teammates two ways: a Targeted Invite straight to one email, or an Open Invite Link anyone on your domain can join through - confirmed automatically, no extra routing needed.

## Line 5 — Suspend & Wake (Frame 5)

**Time:** 28.0 – 35.0s
**Delivery:** Reassuring on "nothing is lost," crisp close.

    Idle instances suspend automatically to save cost - nothing is lost. Visiting the URL won't restart it; a Wake Up action brings it back in a minute or two.
