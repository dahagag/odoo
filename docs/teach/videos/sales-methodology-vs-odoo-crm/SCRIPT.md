# SCRIPT — sales-methodology-vs-odoo-crm

**Voice:** am_michael (Kokoro, local — no API key, per ADR 0008)
**Voice direction:** Calm, precise, consultant-to-consultant. A senior implementer walking a colleague through a mechanism, not a product pitch. No hype, no rising sell. Let the refusal beat (Line 8) be the only place the delivery hardens.

**Factual basis:** every number, label and outcome below is the addon's own seeded demo
data — `custom_addons/crm_methodology/data/crm_methodology_data.xml` (MEDDIC's six
Requirements and three Playbook Questions) and `demo/crm_methodology_demo.xml` (the
opportunity *Nimbus Robotics - Line 2 Expansion*, stage Qualified, with only
`meddic_champion` filled). A viewer can sign into the demo instance and reproduce every
beat. Nothing here is illustrative.

---

## Line 1 — Never heard of it (Frame 1)

**Delivery:** Flat and matter-of-fact. Each denial lands as its own short sentence; let a beat of air sit between them.

    Your team runs MEDDIC. Your CRM has never heard of it. No field. No stage. Nowhere to put it.

## Line 2 — So name it (Frame 2)

**Delivery:** The turn. Slightly warmer — this is the answer to Line 1. Land "holding the deal to it" without pushing it.

    So name it. One record: MEDDIC, Sandler, SPIN — or your own. Attach it to a client, and Odoo starts holding the deal to it.

## Line 3 — What a methodology owns (Frame 3)

**Delivery:** Teaching register. "Block the deal, or just warn" is the pair the whole video turns on — separate the two options clearly.

    A methodology owns two things. Requirements — a qualification field, the moment it's checked, and what happens if it's missing: block the deal, or just warn. And Playbook Questions, tied to an activity.

## Line 4 — Inherited, not chosen (Frame 4)

**Delivery:** Start the walkthrough. "Watch one opportunity" is an invitation — a small lift, then settle.

    Watch one opportunity. The client carries the methodology by default, so every new opportunity inherits it on creation — inherited, not chosen. A rep can still change it.

## Line 5 — The tab that already knows (Frame 5)

**Delivery:** Three readings in a row, each its own cue. Slow enough that the figure, the warnings and the single blocker each get their own moment. Land "unnamed" quietly — it is the seed of the refusal four beats later.

    The Qualification tab is live. One of six requirements filled. Four gaps that only warn. And one that blocks — the Economic Buyer, still unnamed.

## Line 6 — Warned, not blocked (Frame 6)

**Delivery:** Even and unhurried. This is the control case, not a setback — "flagged, not stopped" is the point, so resist making it sound like a failure.

    So the rep raises a quotation. Two requirements are missing at that checkpoint — but both only warn. The quotation goes through: flagged, not stopped.

## Line 7 — The questions it asks (Frame 7)

**Delivery:** The coaching half. Warm and plain; this is the addon helping rather than gating.

    Mark the call done, and the Playbook Questions surface — the ones tied to that activity. Three questions, asked at exactly the moment they can still change the deal.

## Line 8 — Refused (Frame 8)

**Delivery:** The hero beat. Hold a real pause before "So Odoo refuses." Then the three closing fragments get harder and shorter — "Not a nudge. Not a colored badge. Refused." — with the last one flat and final.

    Then the rep marks it Won. The Economic Buyer is still empty, and at this checkpoint it blocks. So Odoo refuses, and says exactly why. Not a nudge. Not a colored badge. Refused.

## Line 9 — Your pipeline never moved (Frame 9)

**Delivery:** The reassurance a consultant needs. Unhurried through the three "untouched" items, then a clean stop. "A coaching layer with teeth" is the last thing said — let it sit, do not sell it.

    And pull back. Your pipeline never moved. No business logic in the addon reads or writes a stage — your kanban, your views, your automations, untouched. It gates two actions, never your pipeline. A coaching layer with teeth.
