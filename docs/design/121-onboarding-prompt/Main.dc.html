Welcome to your trial

This is a quick orientation to how your trial works - not a product tour. Here's what to know before you dive in.

Seats
Your trial has a fixed seat cap. Once a teammate joins, they take one of those seats.
Expiry & extension
Your trial expires on a set date, shown in the systray up top. Ask your sales contact for an extension before it lapses.
Inviting teammates
You can invite teammates yourself, up to the seat cap - no need to route it through factory1.
Suspend & Wake
Idle instances suspend automatically to save cost. A "Wake Up" action brings it back in a minute or two.
